FAQ

The car falls off the ramp; I can't hang upside down.

Press SUCTION before the ramp and never let go; the fans hold the car to the surface. At the top press BRAKE to hang, release SUCTION when ready, and drag left/right while falling to land on the wheels.

Tilt steering is too sensitive / doesn't work.

Turn TILT off and on in Settings. However you hold the phone during the countdown counts as "straight". iOS asks for motion permission the first time.

The game stutters.

Low Power Mode caps the frame rate at 30; try turning it off. Closing heavy background apps helps too.

My progress is gone.

Progress is stored only on the device; deleting the app deletes it. There is no cloud backup yet.
